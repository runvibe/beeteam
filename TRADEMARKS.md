## Independent forks and services

Choose a distinct product name and branding for a publicly distributed modified version or an independently branded hosted service. You may identify its origin with a truthful attribution such as "based on Bee Team," while clearly identifying its independent operator. Retain required copyright, license and attribution notices; rebranding is not a reason to remove them.

Without separate written permission, do not use Bee Team marks as your own product identity, claim official status or certification, imply affiliation or endorsement, or use confusing names, logos or domains for an independent product or service.

Running an official instance for your own team does not require rebranding. A truthful description of an independently operated instance is permitted when it clearly identifies the operator and does not imply official hosting or endorsement.

This policy concerns the Bee Team identity and the specifically identified brand artwork. It does not claim exclusive rights over hexagons, monochrome palettes, general interface layouts, common geometric shapes, open-source components, third-party fonts or third-party icons.
