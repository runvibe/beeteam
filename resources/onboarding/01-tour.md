# Tour the Bee Team panel

Get to know the main areas of Bee Team before you create anything.

## Steps

1. Open **Home** to see your overview of boards and recent work.
2. Open **Tasks** to browse every task across your boards.
3. Open **Agent resources** to see the agents available to your teams.
4. Open **Teams** to see how leaders and workers are grouped.
5. Open the **chat panel** and say hello to the Bee Team Guide, the leader of Bee Team HQ.

## Done when

You can find each area from the navigation and the chat panel opens the guide.
