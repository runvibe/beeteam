# Create your first team and its leader

Teams group a leader agent with worker agents around a shared purpose.

## Steps

1. Ask the guide in the chat panel to help you, or open **Agent resources** and create the agent that will lead the team.
2. Open **Teams** and create a team with a name, a purpose and that leader.
3. Pick the role icons you want for the leader and the workers.

## Done when

Your new team appears in **Teams** and you can open its leader in the chat panel.
