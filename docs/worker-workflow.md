# Worker workflow

Start with the [complete worker workflow and Mermaid diagrams](../skills/get-a-worker/references/worker-workflow.md). This is the maintained English source for preparation, execution, decisions, delivery and resumption.

A worker confirms its persisted identity and membership before accepting work. It reads the assigned task and the exact comment cited by the lead, confirms its scope and plan, and records the execution start with measured session evidence. It records results and blockers on the task before notifying the lead by task and comment ID. The lead handles human decisions and acceptance. [Follow a worked example](guides/first-team.md).
